**Short description (80 chars max):**

**Full description:**

Load the trucks and keep the dock clear in this relaxing color sorting puzzle!

Tap a stack to send its top crate to the truck of the same color. No matching truck? The crate waits on the dock. When a truck is full it drives away and the next one pulls up. Plan your taps so the dock never fills up.

HOW TO PLAY
- Tap a stack to move its top crate
- Crates go straight to a truck of the same color
- Other crates wait on the dock until their truck arrives
- Ship every truck to clear the level

FEATURES
- Easy to learn, satisfying to master
- Endless levels that add more colors and trucks as you go
- Every level is guaranteed to be solvable
- Undo, Hint and extra dock slots when you get stuck
- Short levels, perfect for a quick break
- Play offline, no internet needed
- Small download, runs smoothly on any phone

Can you ship them all?
